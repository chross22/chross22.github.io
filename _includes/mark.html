{%- comment -%} Small marks: two of Camille's pencil cartoons traced to vector, and a set of glyphs that reduce the /code/ schematics to icon scale. Everything here draws in currentColor and var(--accent), so one file serves both themes and both sizes — there is no second export and no white raster panel to glare on the dark theme. include.name — copepod | whale | gulf-map tau-patch | npp | dsm | detection | regime-shift | swell include.class — mark--section (beside a heading) | mark--term (glossary) include.label — accessible name. Omit where the neighbouring text already names the thing, and the mark is hidden from assistive tech rather than announced twice. The glyphs share a 0 0 32 32 box and a 1.6 stroke so they read as one set at 32px. The two traced drawings are fills, not strokes, and carry their own ink-tight viewBox — see the note on .mark--term for why 32px is their floor. gulf-map is real Natural Earth coastline, not a freehand approximation: the Gulf only reads if the Cape Cod hook, Penobscot Bay and the Bay of Fundy resolve, and that needs the actual geometry at real fidelity. An earlier pass simplified it twice over and the coast came out as an angular wire — hence the fine tolerance here. It is drawn as a framed inset: land carries a flat tint at coarse fidelity because it is only a wash, the coastline is fine because it is the line you actually see, and the border contains the land where it runs off the window instead of letting it read as a slab. Rings are cut open where they met the crop box, so no frame edge is ever stroked as though it were coast. Each
